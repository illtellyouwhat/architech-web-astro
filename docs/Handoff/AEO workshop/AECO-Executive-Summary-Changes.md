# AECO Strategy: Executive Summary & Proposed Changes

## Executive Summary (200 words)

Answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) are fundamentally changing B2B service discovery. Unlike traditional search where users click through to evaluate vendors, answer engines synthesize information and may never send prospects to your site. Your current content uses generic technical terminology ("LLM applications," "data pipelines") that lacks semantic hooks for answer engines to match with prospect queries like "How do I automate clinical trial patient matching?" or "What's the ROI of production scheduling automation?"

This analysis proposes restructuring content to be **question-first, outcome-oriented, and industry-specific**. Key changes: (1) Hero section rewrite using prospect questions with embedded metrics and industry terminology, (2) Industry cards using insider vocabulary (e.g., "e-discovery" not "legal document processing"), (3) Case study restructuring with Quick Facts sections for structured data extraction, (4) Service cards reframed as specific problems solved rather than capability categories. 

The urgency: answer engines are training on web content now. Early adopters of answer engine optimization capture "authority" positioning that compounds over 6-12 months. Without these changes, competitors with inferior execution but better content structure will win AI-mediated searches. The fix requires reorganizing existing strong content, not creating net-new capabilities.

## Measurement Framework

**Direct Indicators:**
- Featured snippet capture (Google/Perplexity citations)
- Question-query traffic ("how to," "what is," "should I")
- Zero-click search volume (content cited without click-through)

**Indirect Indicators:**
- Referral traffic from AI chat interfaces
- Case study bounce rate (lower = better intent match)
- Time-to-contact (shorter = better pre-qualification)

**Competitive Indicators:**
- Share of voice in AI summaries vs competitors
- Citation quality (vendor option vs category authority)
